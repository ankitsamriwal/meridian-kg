# BRD - Falcon Retail CRM Programme (v1.2, approved)

Prepared by Ankit Samriwal (Alpha Data). Reviewed and approved by Sara Ahmed and Mohammed Al Farsi (Falcon Retail Group).

## Business problem
Falcon Retail Group's 84-store operation runs customer data across a legacy CRM (end-of-life, vendor support ends March 2027), an e-commerce platform, and loyalty spreadsheets maintained by the marketing team. No single customer view exists; B2B gifting quotes are built in Excel.

## Business objectives
- BO-1: Single customer profile across all touchpoints before the December 2026 peak season.
- BO-2: Reduce quote turnaround for B2B gifting from 2 days to under 4 hours.
- BO-3: Retire the legacy CRM before vendor support ends.
- BO-4: Loyalty tier history preserved and actionable for win-back campaigns (Layla Haddad, sponsor).

## Scope statement
In scope: D365 Sales, Customer Insights, loyalty data migration, e-commerce sync middleware, Power BI executive dashboards. Out of scope: POS replacement, ERP financials, mobile app.
