# Proposal: Falcon Retail Group - Dynamics 365 Sales & Customer Insights

Prepared by Alpha Data for Falcon Retail Group. July 2026.

## Executive summary
Falcon Retail Group operates 84 stores across the GCC with customer data fragmented across a legacy CRM, an e-commerce platform, and spreadsheet-based loyalty tracking. Alpha Data proposes a Dynamics 365 Sales and Customer Insights implementation to unify customer profiles, modernise the sales pipeline, and give Falcon a single view of the customer ahead of the 2026 peak season.

## Scope
- Dynamics 365 Sales: lead-to-order pipeline for the B2B gifting division and key-account management.
- Customer Insights: unification of ~212,000 contact records from three source systems.
- Loyalty data model: migration of spreadsheet loyalty tracking into Customer Insights.
- Integration middleware (falcon-crm-sync) for near-real-time sync with the e-commerce platform.
- Power BI executive dashboards for commercial leadership.
