# Engagement history

Falcon Retail Group ran an RFP in May 2026 for their CRM modernisation; four SIs bid. Alpha Data won on the strength of the loyalty-migration approach - Ankit Samriwal led the presales response and the orals presentation to Mohammed Al Farsi and Sara Ahmed on 9 June.

The predecessor system is a legacy CRM the previous SI installed in 2019; it goes end-of-support in March 2027, which is why the board set the December 2026 deadline for the new platform.

Contract signed 15 July 2026 (AED 1.45M fixed price). Priya Nair staffed as full-time PM from 3 August; Daniel Osei at 75%; Rishabh Kapoor full-time on data migration. Meera Krishnan (QA Lead) joined in September as test scope firmed up.

Relationship note: Alpha Data previously delivered Falcon's e-commerce replatform in 2024 - Sara Ahmed was the client lead then too, which is why the integration approach through falcon-crm-sync was an easy sell.
